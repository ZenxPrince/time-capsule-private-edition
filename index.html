01 / THE IDEA

Not everything
needs to be shared now.

Some thoughts deserve a little distance. Time Capsule gives them a place to wait — sealed on your device, until the date you choose.

No account to create. No server to trust with your words. Just a browser, a password, and a future moment.

05 / SECURITY MODEL

Understand what
protects your words.

Specific primitives. Clear boundaries. No claim of perfect security.

A / ENCRYPTION256bit

AES-GCM

Authenticated encryption using a 256-bit key, with a fresh 12-byte IV for each capsule.

B / KEY DERIVATION210k

PBKDF2 · SHA-256

210,000 iterations and a unique 16-byte salt are used to derive the capsule key from its password.

C / PERSISTENCE0 servers

LocalStorage

The app stores encrypted capsule data in this browser. It does not synchronize between devices.

D / INTEGRITYSHA256

Ciphertext fingerprint

A fingerprint is recalculated on opening for comparison. AES-GCM performs the message authentication check.
